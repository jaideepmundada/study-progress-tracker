
# StudySync – Full-Stack Syllabus & Unit Progress Tracker

## 📚 Project Overview

StudySync is a full-stack web application designed to help students manage their academic syllabus and track their study progress unit by unit.

The application allows students to organize subjects, view their syllabus, monitor completed and pending units, and track their overall academic progress. It provides a simple and user-friendly dashboard that helps students plan their studies effectively and complete their syllabus on time.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Organize subjects and their respective units.
- Track completed, pending, and ongoing syllabus units.
- Monitor overall syllabus completion percentage.
- Help students manage their study schedules.
- Improve time management and productivity.
- Provide a centralized platform for academic progress tracking.

## ✨ Features

1. **User Authentication**
   - Student registration and login.
   - Secure user authentication.
   - Individual student dashboards.

2. **Subject Management**
   - Add, edit, and delete subjects.
   - Organize subjects semester-wise.
   - View subject details.

3. **Syllabus Management**
   - Add units under each subject.
   - View unit-wise syllabus.
   - Update syllabus information.

4. **Progress Tracking**
   - Mark units as completed or pending.
   - Track subject-wise progress.
   - Display overall syllabus completion percentage.

5. **Dashboard**
   - Display total subjects.
   - Show completed and pending units.
   - Visualize academic progress.
   - Provide a quick overview of study activities.
